# Introduction

These articles are the law of A New Republic.

The nation is limited. People govern the limits directly, or through delegates they can dismiss at any time. People who want more services, subsidies, or taxes than the nation allows may form a [sub-jurisdiction](federalism.md) and try them there. Capturing a national office is a poor prize, because a national office can do very little.

## What is law

1. This constitution.
2. [Statutes](../statutes/introduction.md) enacted under the [legislature](legislature.md) article.
3. A [charter](../sub-jurisdiction/introduction.md) and the local statutes under it, inside that sub-jurisdiction's territory, where they respect this constitution.

The signed commit named by the [governance record](definitions.md) is the text. A website, a company that hosts the repository, and an official's announcement are copies. A judgment is not a fourth text. It binds the parties to the case, under the [judiciary](judiciary.md) article. The court holds no key that merges or deletes a commit.

A treaty is law only for the outward bargain it states, and only as the [defense](defense.md) article ranks it. It sits below this constitution and below a statute. An ordinary ballot can enact a treaty. It cannot amend this constitution by doing so.

The authoritative language is English.

Commentary, forum polls, campaign statements, and explanations produced by a person or a machine are not law. Where the words of a statute and the words of this constitution cannot both be obeyed in a case, this constitution is obeyed in that case. The statute stays on the enforced branch until the voters change it.

Code that checks a proposal or counts a ballot implements a rule written here. In the case before it, a court may refuse to apply a machine result when the machine has departed from the rule. A reorganization of the ledger does not repeal a statute after the finality rule in the cryptographic specification has been met.

## How change works

Ordinary statutes change on the consent calendar or by an ordinary ballot, after a public review, as the legislature article describes. The constitution changes only by a constitutional ballot. Scope in particular does not grow by interpretation, by treaty, by emergency, or by a grant with a string attached.

Statutes expire unless renewed, except the short list of enduring statutes in the legislature article. The constitution does not expire by sunset.

## What force exists

The [executive](executive.md) carries out the law and does not write it. The [courts](judiciary.md) decide cases and do not write it. Juries decide guilt. Delegates cast votes and hold no office. There is no president and no legislative house.

Officers who must exist are drawn by lot, serve a single term, and do not return. They serve from where they live.

## Founding

The founding commit enacts this constitution and any statute it contains. It may name an initial council and initial judges, who serve only until the first sortition and for no more than one year. It may record the adults who took the oath as the first citizens.

The first council is drawn by lot within one year after 50 adult citizens hold credentials. Initial officers cannot amend this constitution, create a crime, or spend beyond receipts already in hand.

# Scope of the nation

The nation keeps the peace and protects life and physical property inside its borders. It is not the manager of livelihoods, beliefs, or local experiments.

## The nation may

- Protect persons and physical property inside the borders from force, fraud, and invasion.
- Make treaties limited to peace, trade, and alliance, under the [defense](defense.md) article.
- Keep courts, the citizenship credential, the land records, and the legislative machinery this constitution describes.
- Levy the [land tax](money.md).
- Lay a security tariff, or an import or export control, only as the [defense](defense.md) article allows, and not as a general power over trade.
- Punish [national crimes](criminal.md), and hear the civil cases this constitution describes.
- Control entry of non-citizens under the defense article.

A power has to fit one of those sentences. A court reads them narrowly. An ambiguity is resolved against the power.

## The nation does not

Anything omitted above is outside national power. The following are named because other republics have used them as the path back to a general government. Omitting some further power from this list does not smuggle it in.

- Education, health care, housing, food, income support, and care of the poor.
- The wages, hours, and organization of private work.
- Prices, quality rules, occupational licenses, and tariffs or trade bans that are not the security controls in the defense article.
- The content or carriage of private communication networks.
- Planning of production, except that a person who damages another's person or property answers for the damage.
- Conditional grants to sub-jurisdictions.
- Binding rules issued by officers. Binding national rules are statutes.

The nation does not do indirectly what it cannot do directly. An emergency, a treaty, a criminal charge, a spending condition, and a "guidance" document are not extra powers.

## Changing the scope

Adding a power, or striking a limit in this article, is a constitutional change. A super-majority on the constitutional ballot is required, twice.

Sub-jurisdictions may exercise powers the nation does not have. They do it under the [federalism](federalism.md) article, and they do not drag the nation along with them.

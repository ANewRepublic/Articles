# Threat model

> [!NOTE]
> This file is commentary. It is not law. Where a mitigation is required, the requirement is in the constitution or a statute. This note says why it is there.

## Vote buying and coercion

A buyer needs proof. The required property is that a citizen can see their own ballot counted and cannot hand that proof to anyone else. Replacing the ballot until the close blunts a watcher who sees one vote and then leaves. A private terminal blunts a watcher who would stand in the room. A device-bound credential blunts "give me your key."

A coercer who can watch someone for the entire window, everywhere, is still a problem. The law treats that conduct as the crime. It does not pretend the math alone defeats a jailer in the house. Public delegates are different: their ballots are public on purpose, and secret payments to them are a crime, because cryptography cannot make a powerful delegate both accountable and invisible.

## Sneak passage on the consent calendar

Silence enacts on the consent calendar, and that calendar cannot carry a crime, a tax, a treaty, a renewal, or a constitutional change. The mitigations are the frozen public diff, 90 days, free daily comments, and an objection threshold of 5 percent. A single public delegate is not 5 percent. Several of them, or a modest group voting directly, can force a real ballot. Analyses by models are useful here and are not authorities. A captured explainer that says "this is clerical" does not make it clerical. The diff does.

## Whale delegates

Liquid democracy concentrates weight. There is no cap, because forbidding a citizen to trust one person recreates a parliament. Past the public-delegate threshold the ballot and the total weight are public, pay is only the open channel, and the delegator list stays secret so the whale cannot prove a following to a buyer. A delegate's vote has to sit visible for seven days before it binds, and the delegator can replace it until the close. That week is the practical remedy for an unfaithful delegate. Instant revocation remains the remedy for the next proposal.

## The platform and the ledger

A host can ban a user, rewrite a branch, or lose the servers. The host is a mirror. The governance record names the commit. No single operator holds the key that enacts. A fork after finality does not change the law. Courts, not miners, resolve a disputed record.

## Sortition

If the seed is chosen after the pool is known, the lot is an appointment. The seed is committed first. Hardship exemptions are judged by a separate drawn panel so the council cannot stock itself. Compulsory short service is there so the pool is not only people who want power. It is also a burden, which is why the term is one year, remote, and excused for hardship.

## Residency games

Moving for one ballot, or visiting once a year to keep a national vote, packs the eligible weight with people who do not live under the result. Weight on a proposal requires citizenship and residence already in force when review started, and a residence change waits 90 days.

## Agents

An agent that holds a credential is a stolen vote that scales. Credentials do not export. Agents may draft and may explain. They do not vote. A person who directs an agent to commit a crime is the principal.

## Emergencies and offices

The usual death of a republic is an emergency that never ends, or an office that writes "temporary" rules. Decrees do not merge into the law. Defense of the territory during an actual attack is the narrow exception, and it adds no domestic power. The council has no veto and no power to ignore a statute. The prosecutor is not the council's lawyer.

## Assessment

"Perceived value" in the hands of an appointed assessor is a quiet tax set by a political class. The formula is public, improvements are outside it, a citizen panel can cut an inflated value, and a real sale can cut it further. The nation does not have to buy the parcel. That stronger discipline is an open question because it turns the treasury into a land fund.

## Local majorities

A town cannot vote a holdout's parcel in, and cannot draw a ring around it. The founders own the land they include, and an unwilling owner stays out unless they sell. The cost is that a map may have a hole, or the founders may have to choose a different outline. An enclosing town may sue if the outline cuts emergency and defense access, and it loses that suit if a passage right is recorded. It does not get a political veto, which would be how large towns freeze the map.

## Courts that unwrite a ballot

A court that can delete a voted text, or that can grow a second constitution out of opinions, holds a power the tally was supposed to keep. Sortition makes that worse if one drawn judge can do it. The mitigation is mechanical. The court has no merge key. The enacted commit stays until a later tally changes it. One judge decides the parties in the room. A citation to an earlier case exists only when a unanimous national panel tied the reading to one statute, and only until that statute sunsets, is repealed, or is renewed. Renewal of the same words still ends it, unless the voters write the reading into the new text. Readings of the constitution do not bind the next court. A local judge does not order the national council or a national officer to stop. Motive review of a tariff or a frontier list is not a judicial power. The political checks are the sunset, the renewal, and the grave ballot.

## Treaties that outrank the frame

A bargain that is easier to pass than an amendment, and is then treated as harder to break than the amendment, lets a foreign promise rewrite the frame. Treaties take an ordinary ballot, they sunset, and they lose to both the constitution and a statute. An officer does not get to say the nation's word has tied its hands past the text.

## Secret courts

Probable cause of a named offense, stated in a warrant from an ordinary judge, is the path into a person's privacy. A standing secret docket, hearing only the government, is the path that becomes a rubber stamp. Delayed notice ends when the destruction risk ends, and the warrant is then public. Intelligence collection that touches a person here uses that same door.

## Disarmament by rumor

Keeping arms is not evidence of illness, and a neighbor's campaign is not a judgment. A mental-condition disability takes a national jury, a present likelihood of unlawful violence, and an end date within a year. A petition that invents acts, or that treats gun ownership as the illness, is itself a national crime. Criminal disabilities are a closed list of national violent offenses. The seller learns only a zero-knowledge proof of eligibility, so the check does not become a registry of buyers. Town convictions never set the flag. Violent conduct that should disarm has to be on the national list, not left for each town to define.

## The high bar itself

Constitutional error is hard to repair. That includes errors in this draft. The bar is the protection against a weekend majority. It is also a reason to keep the constitution short and to put numbers that should move into statutes.

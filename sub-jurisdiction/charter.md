# Charter pattern

> [!NOTE]
> This file is a pattern, not a charter in force. A real charter is adopted by the residents of specific land. If this pattern and the [federalism](../constitution/federalism.md) article differ, the article governs.

Replace the bracketed words. Delete services the founders do not want. Do not delete the guarantees.

## 1. Name and land

This charter governs [name], the connected parcels listed by id in the founding map. Every owner of those parcels has consented. The map does not surround an owner who has not joined. Land outside that list is not governed by this charter. A recorded right of passage for defense and emergency aid, if the national court required one, is part of the map.

## 2. Guarantees

The national constitution binds this territory. In particular, and without narrowing the rest of it:

- national rights apply here in full
- a person leaves with personal property, with no exit tax and no detention for leaving
- this charter does not borrow, does not issue money, and does not make anything legal tender
- this charter does not conscript, does not establish a church, and does not compel support of one
- the only civic oath is the national oath. This charter asks no oath of loyalty, residence, or silence
- officers [must be members of a named religion / need not be]. That requirement applies to officers only
- private worship is lawful here, including a congregation meeting on private land it has a right to use. Public ceremonies are [named / not prescribed]. Public land and public funds are [open to every faith / reserved to the named religion / not used for worship]
- government schools, private schools, and home education are treated equally if education is funded
- officers do not make law, and local courts do not order national officers
- local statutes and local taxes sunset within the national sunset limit unless renewed by an ordinary ballot. A local judgment binds the parties. It is not precedent
- each resident citizen has one vote. [Other citizens who own land here also have one vote. / No other person has a vote.] No person has more than one. A non-citizen has none
- no tax reaches wealth, a bank balance, a financial holding, or an act performed outside this territory
- a jail or courtroom forbids arms inside only while armed guards staff a gun-detection booth at each public entrance

## 3. Powers

This people adds only the following powers: [list].

A power not listed does not exist here. Adding a power or a tax is a constitutional ballot of the citizens who hold a vote under this charter. Repealing one is an ordinary ballot or the consent calendar.

## 4. Taxes and services

[Name each tax, its base, its rate, and the service or subsidy it pays for.]

Taxes are prospective. They reach acts performed inside this territory and property situated here. They do not reach a wage earned outside, a purchase made outside, or a balance. Unpaid local tax is collected by suit or by lien on land that remains, not by jail and not at the border.

Programs create no right to continue.

## 5. Peace and local crimes

[Name local offenses and civil penalties, if any.]

No local term of incarceration exceeds the national local cap. A jury is unanimous and has 12 members whenever incarceration is possible. Protected speech, worship, status, and the act of leaving are not offenses. An act performed outside this territory is not an offense here. Public exposure, and public display of an indecent image, may be named below. A visitor is held to a local offense other than force, theft, fraud, or trespass only when that rule is posted, in words an ordinary adult can read on the spot, where the act happens or at each public road entrance.

## 6. Officers

[Name any officers, how they are chosen, and the length of a single term.]

Officers carry out this charter and the local statutes. They do not write them, do not suspend them, and do not bind the nation. The national council is not our executive, and our officers are not the nation's.

## 7. Repository

Local law lives in a public repository. Enactment follows the national legislature article, using this territory's eligible weight. National text is a read-only input.

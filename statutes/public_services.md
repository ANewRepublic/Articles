# Public services

National services are the duties in the [scope](../constitution/scope.md) article: courts, defense, enrollment, the legislative record, and the land records. Anything else is not a national service.

## Who pays

A service one person can consume, leaving less for others, is paid by the people who use it. A toll, a filing fee, and a charge for an expedited record are lawful when a statute states them. The price may move with scarcity. No fee is charged for habeas corpus, for the private voting terminal, for renewal of citizenship, or for the enrollment fee when it has been waived. Other fees are waived for a person who cannot pay them.

The indivisible duties, defense and the courts in particular, are paid by the land tax. They are not divided into user fees that would let an attacker or a wealthy litigant buy the peace.

Counsel required by the rights article, and the per diems of jurors and council members, are national duties paid by the land tax, not by the accused and not by the juror.

## Life-preserving quantities

Where the nation administers a natural resource because it lies under national land, a life-preserving quantity of water is available to persons at cost, and further consumption is priced. The nation does not administer water on private land or on a sub-jurisdiction's land. A charter may make its own rule for its own resources, including subsidies, under the federalism article.

## Roads and other works

A national road exists only as a defense or court-access need stated in a statute, and its users may be tolled. Local roads are local. A sub-jurisdiction may subsidize them or toll them.

The nation does not build works in order to employ people or to favor a place. A work that is not required by an enumerated duty fails the scope article even if a majority wants it.

## What this statute does not fund

Education, libraries, health care, housing, food, and income are not national services. A sub-jurisdiction may provide them as programs. The nation does not. User fees and the land tax are the whole national funding story. There is no third fund.

# Statutes

Statutes are ordinary national law. They are enacted by the [legislature](../constitution/legislature.md) article and they expire unless renewed, except the four enduring statutes named there:

- [Land tax](taxation.md), which a failed renewal does not repeal. A frontier surcharge is a separate ordinary statute and can lapse.
- [National crimes](crimes.md)
- [Incarceration](incarceration.md)
- [Cryptographic specification](cryptography.md)

[Public services](public_services.md) and [production and waste](production.md) are ordinary. If they lapse, the constitutional duties and the civil right to sue for damage remain. The fee schedules and the disposal details do not.

A statute that contradicts the constitution is not law.

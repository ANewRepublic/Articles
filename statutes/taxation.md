# Land tax

The nation taxes land under the [money](../constitution/money.md) article. This statute is the formula. It is enduring. A renewal is debated at each sunset, and a failed renewal leaves the formula in force. Setting the rate at zero, or repealing the formula, takes a grave ballot. Any other change to the rate is an ordinary ballot.

## Why land

* WHEREAS the government's chief job is to protect life and property.
* WHEREAS the cost of that protection tracks the land under protection.
* THEREFORE land is taxed on unimproved value and on the cost of protection.

Size enters the formula only by changing unimproved value or the cost of protection. Improvements are outside the base. A barn, a crop, and a repaired roof do not raise the national tax.

The incidence of a land tax on the price of goods is discussed in the [commentary](../commentary/incidence.md). That note is not part of the formula.

## Formula

Until a later enacted amendment states numbers, the ordinary rate is the rate in the founding commit. If the founding commit states none, the rate is zero and no national land tax is collected until a renewal states one. A zero rate is a lawful formula. It is a poor way to fund the duties in the scope article, and the people can change it by an ordinary proposal.

The assessment of a parcel is its unimproved value multiplied by the ordinary rate, or by the frontier rate when the parcel is on an enacted frontier list.

Unimproved value is taken from arm's-length sales of comparable parcels, adjusted only for location, size, and the defense-cost class. The adjustment method is published with the assessment. An owner, an improvement, and a name are not inputs.

## Frontier list

The council may propose a list of border parcels, each with a written reason that defense of that parcel costs materially more. The list is a separate ordinary statute. It is not part of this enduring formula, and it lapses at its sunset unless an ordinary ballot renews it. When it lapses, those parcels pay the ordinary rate. The frontier rate is at most twice the ordinary rate. The voters accept or reject the written reason by enacting or refusing the list. A court applies the list's words. It does not remove a parcel because it doubts the motive.

## Appeal

An owner may appeal an assessment to a panel of six citizens drawn by lot, within 90 days of publication.

If the panel finds the assessment more than 15 percent above the value the comparable sales support, it replaces the assessment and the treasury refunds the excess for that year.

If the owner sells the parcel at arm's length within one year for less than the assessed value, the assessment for that year is reset to the sale price and the excess is refunded. A sale to a relative, to a principal of the owning entity, or to a buyer the court finds is a straw buyer does not reset the value.

## Payment and default

Payment is a proof that meets the money article. The method of testing an instrument against those tests is published as an ordinary statute. Until that statute exists, a court may find that a particular proof meets the tests. The council does not pick a favorite instrument in the meantime. The governance record stores the proof against the parcel id. It does not publish the owner.

The land is the only security. There is no deficiency judgment and no claim against personal property for the national land tax.

Failure to pay leads to auction of that parcel.

- Notice goes to the owner through the commitment-opening a court authorizes for this purpose, and notice is posted against the parcel id for 90 days.
- The starting bid is the unpaid tax and the costs of the sale.
- Any citizen or legal entity may bid. A bid that would put title in a non-citizen, or in an entity with a non-citizen beneficial owner, is void.
- The price above the starting bid is paid to the owner.
- If no one bids, the nation takes the parcel in satisfaction of that tax and offers it again within one year on the same terms. Until resale the council does nothing with the parcel except keep third parties from seizing it. The nation is not a landlord and does not develop the parcel.
- A second failure to sell leaves the parcel as national land under that same minimal rule until a later auction succeeds.

A bid-rigging agreement, or a council member bidding through a hidden principal, is fraud against the nation's processes.

## Records

Public records for each parcel are geometry, size, assessment, rate, formula inputs, lien amounts, and sale prices with dates.

Owner identity, principal identity, and payment history beyond the bare fact that the parcel is current or in default are private under the privacy article. "In default" is public because the auction cannot be secret. The name still is not.
